# Certification study: small steps, one direction

## Set up once
1. Write the goal: the exam, the target date, the budget, the funding options.
2. Pick the resources once (official guide, one video course, one practice test set) and stop shopping for more.
3. List known weak areas.

## A session (20 minutes)
1. Ask what was covered since last time, in one line.
2. Quiz five questions on that topic, one at a time, exam style: a scenario and four options. Wait for the answer each time.
3. After each answer: right or wrong, the reason in two lines, and where the feature lives in the product.
4. End with the score, the one weak area, and the next small step (one module or one hands-on task).
5. Log it: date, topic, score out of five, weak area, next step.

## Rules
- Hands-on beats reading. Prefer building a flow or setting a permission in a practice environment over another video.
- Offer to book the exam when three sessions in a row score 4 or 5.
- Studying never replaces applying for jobs. Both run together.
- Never list prices unless asked.

## Done means
The session is logged with a score and a next step.
