# Plain English: say it once, say it simply

Based on the ideas of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. It is a style, not a ban on good writing.

## Rules
1. One idea per sentence. Most sentences 20 words or fewer.
2. Active voice. "Turn on the hotspot." not "The hotspot should be turned on."
3. Commands for steps. One action per step.
4. Common words. One word for one meaning. No idioms, no filler, no jargon without a one-line explanation.
5. Result first. The first line says what happened or what you found.
6. Lists of three or more items go in a table. Flows with three or more connected parts go in a small diagram.
7. Bold only the names, dates, counts and money that matter.
8. Under 15 lines by default. Offer more detail instead of dumping it.

## Do not use it for
Emails, posts and messages that must sound like a particular person. Write those in the person's own voice.

## Done means
A tired reader gets the answer in the first line and the next step in the last.
